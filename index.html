Google MCP (Local)

A personal, self-hosted connector for Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive.

What it does

Google MCP (Local) is a small program that runs only on its owner's own computer. It lets an AI assistant (Claude Code and Claude Desktop) read and act on the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive data across several Google accounts at once, using the Model Context Protocol.

Capabilities: search and read email, create drafts, send and reply; list, create and update calendar events and respond to invitations; search, read, create, update and share Drive files.

Who it is for

This is a personal-use application. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner. Each Google account connected to it belongs to the owner and is connected by the owner through Google's standard sign-in and consent screen.

Data handling

All data stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any third-party server other than Google's own APIs. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
